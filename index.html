WHY DO WE NEED 1 YEAR IS + 2 YEARS BS?

  • ✓ To calculate changes in Balance Sheet items, we need a Beginning Balance and an Ending Balance.
  • ✓ That is why we utilize 2 consecutive years of the Balance Sheet alongside the current Income Statement.

CASH FLOW STATEMENT – 3 MAIN ACTIVITIES

⚙️

1. OPERATING (CFO)

Cash generated or used from core business operations.

Use Inclusions:

• Current Assets (EXCLUDE CASH)

• Current Liabilities (EXCLUDE DEBT & LEASE)

📊

2. INVESTING (CFI)

Cash used or received from buying or selling long-term assets.

Use Inclusions:

• Non-Current Assets

(PP&E, Intangibles, Long-term Investments)

🏛️

3. FINANCING (CFF)

Cash from or paid to capital providers (debt & equity holders).

Use Inclusions:

• Debt, Equity, Dividends, Leases

(As per financial classification)

4

HOW CHANGES AFFECT CASH (OPERATING)

CURRENT ASSETS

Increase ↑Cash Decreases (-)
Decrease ↓Cash Increases (+)
Example: AR increases by 30 → Cash decreases by 30.

CURRENT LIABILITIES

Increase ↑Cash Increases (+)
Decrease ↓Cash Decreases (-)
Example: AP increases by 40 → Cash increases by 40.
5

CFO FORMULA (INDIRECT METHOD)

CFO = Net Income

+ Non-Cash Expenses (e.g., Depreciation)

– Increase in Operating Current Assets

+ Increase in Operating Current Liabilities

💡 Key Idea: We start from Net Income and adjust for non-cash items and working capital changes.
6

CASH FLOW FROM INVESTING (CFI)

Buying Non-Current Assets (PPE, Intangibles) Cash Outflow (-)
Selling Non-Current Assets Cash Inflow (+)

Important PPE Equation:

Ending PPE = Beginning PPE + CapEx – Depreciation – Disposals

7

CASH FLOW FROM FINANCING (CFF)

Borrowing Debt / Issuing Equity

→ Cash Inflow (+)

Repaying Debt / Paying Dividends

→ Cash Outflow (-)

Examples:

• Debt Increase → + Cash

• Debt Decrease → - Cash

• Dividends Paid → - Cash

8

PUTTING IT ALL TOGETHER

CFO FORMULA

Net Income + Non-Cash Items +/- Changes in Operating Working Capital

CFI FORMULA

Purchases / Sales of Non-Current Assets (CapEx)

CFF FORMULA

Debt Issuance/Repayment + Equity Issuance - Dividends

NET CHANGE IN CASH = CFO + CFI + CFF
